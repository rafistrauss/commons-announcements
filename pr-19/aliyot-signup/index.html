🎶 Davening / Leading Tefillah

Leading davening is one of the most meaningful ways to contribute to the Minyan. Even if you only know part of a service, we'd love your help. Morning minyanim are only occasional, so Shacharit opportunities are relatively rare.

📜 Torah Reading (Leining)

Every Shabbat and Yom Tov, we need someone to read from the Torah. Even reading a single aliyah makes a huge difference. In most cases, saying you can lein means committing to lein the 1st aliyah unless arranged otherwise.
